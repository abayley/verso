# ADR-XXXX: Short decision name

## Context

The forces that made a decision necessary: the constraint, the conflict, or the question that had no
settled answer. Written so that someone who was not present can tell why the decision could not be
deferred.

## Decision

The choice, stated as a fact in one paragraph. Write it in the present tense, as something the project
does, not as something it will do.

## Why

The load-bearing reasons, with evidence. Cite measurements, file paths with line numbers, or vendor
specifications where the reason rests on one of them.

## Reopens when

The specific condition that would invalidate this decision. `Never` is a valid entry when the reasoning
is structural rather than circumstantial.

## Consequences

What follows from the decision: the costs accepted, the options now closed, and the work it creates
elsewhere. Omit this section when the decision has no consequence beyond itself.
