A Moebius desert editor in one theme and two hours: the light and dark switch in the top bar re-lights the whole frame, swapping noon for night. By day the sun, birds and dunes sit along the page tree and the document is a cream sheet; by night a starfield fills the chrome, lit crystals stand at the tree's foot, the control bar is a red-rock overhang, and the document goes dark with the world -- the night sky's own colour, with the campfire only catching its rim. Terracotta warms to ember for the active page and mark, turquoise cools to crystal blue for the switches, and the drawn hand -- the wobbling contour and the clumped speckle -- is the same in both hours.

One theme, two hours. Designs R and S are the same Moebius desert at noon and at night, so they are one theme and one scheme switch, not two themes. Use the sun, moon, and monitor buttons at the top right: the scene swaps with the hour, and the page goes dark with the world. Every colour below the two token blocks in the stylesheet is a role name, which is what a real theme would need.
