Mockup L: a Bauhaus / constructivist composition, updated for the current editor and borrowing from Mondrian's neoplasticism. Primary red, blue, and yellow with black rules of deliberately unequal weight on warm off-white; publish and cancel slabs, mode, width, theme, and scheme switches in the ruled header; the page tree as a left rail ending in a Mondrian panel; contents, attachments, and history as a right rail; the command bar as colour-coded geometric tiles; and a Broadway Boogie Woogie segmented strip as the bottom edge.
