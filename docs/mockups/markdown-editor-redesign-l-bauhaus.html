Mockup L: a Bauhaus / constructivist composition, laid out as the editor now is and borrowing from Mondrian's neoplasticism. Primary red, blue, and yellow with black rules of deliberately unequal weight on warm off-white; publish and cancel slabs with the mode, width, theme, and scheme switches in the ruled top bar, the breadcrumb and page search on the row beneath, the page tree as a full-height left rail ending in a Mondrian panel, contents, attachments, and history as a right rail, the command bar as colour-coded geometric tiles, and a Broadway Boogie Woogie segmented strip as the bottom edge. The type is Futura, falling back to Century Gothic and then Avenir Next: the geometric sans of the school itself, and the design depends on it -- the display capitals lose their character in an ordinary grotesque.
