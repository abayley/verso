Mockup R: a Jean Giraud / Moebius desert, laid out as the editor now is. Fine sepia contour lines instead of black, flat colour in hard airbrushed sky bands, a banded sun and drifting birds over the full-height page tree, dunes at its foot, floating crystals over the document, and terracotta and turquoise picking out the active controls. The type is Gill Sans, falling back to Avenir Next and then Trebuchet MS: a humanist sans with calligraphic bones, so its letter-spaced capitals read as BD hand-lettering rather than as a user-interface label.
