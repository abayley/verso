Mockup S: a Moebius crystal night, laid out as the editor now is. Deep ultramarine chrome under a stippled starfield, indigo ink lines instead of black, a red-rock overhang as the control bar, and one fire-lit parchment page glowing in the middle of the dark; a haloed moon and a scatter of stars hang over the page tree with lit crystals at its foot, the active page and controls hold the ember orange of the campfire, and crystal blue picks out the switches. The type is Optima, falling back to Candara and then Gill Sans: a glyphic humanist with flared strokes, so the capitals read as cut and lit like the crystals rather than typeset.
