Mockup T: a Moebius moonlit meadow, laid out as the editor now is. A grey-blue field under a starred slate sky, with a slate-navy ink line instead of black and pink-terracotta for the cypress canopies; the frame is filled with the clearing's pattern of soft scrub tufts, a haloed moon and stars sit over the page tree with scalloped cloud-trees at its foot, every control is a rounded pill, the terracotta pink marks the active page, and one saturated swallow blue is spent only on the active mark. The type is Seravek, falling back to Segoe UI and then Trebuchet MS: a soft humanist sans whose open, rounded bowls sit with the meadow's scalloped forms.
