Mockup U: a Moebius sandcastle city under a noon sky, after Norah's Home, laid out as the editor now is. Cerulean sky with cream cumulus, warm brown ink, apricot and rose towers with verdigris shrubbery and a slate pyramid; the page tree rail climbs to a spired skyline at its foot, gulls drift over the pages, the top bar is a cloud band, and the document stands in the sky as the pale sandstone tower, its grain gathering toward its base. The type is Roboto, the app's own face, used outright: this design tests the identity carried by colour and drawing alone, with the production font.
