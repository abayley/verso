Mockup V: a Moebius coral maze, after the giant shell painting, laid out as the editor now is. A densely worked blush coral field fills the frame -- rings of growth in aubergine ink, speckled like the etched ground -- and every working surface is a clean paved walkway laid onto it, edged with the painting's teal channel and gold kerb: the top bar, the document, the side panels, and the command bar. Vermilion, the shell's colour, is the one hot accent, spent on the active page and the active mark, with its ornament dotted into the active row. The type is Roboto, the app's own face, used outright: this design tests the identity carried by colour and drawing alone, with the production font.
